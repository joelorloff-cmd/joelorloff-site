I am an animation director and illustrator living in Brooklyn, New York. I've led animation teams for a number of award-winning documentary projects, including Oren Rudavsky's film Elie Wiesel: Soul on Fire, Pam Hogan's film The Day Iceland Stood Still and American Masters' Unladylike2020, among others. I won best music video for Pink Floyd's Breathe in their 50th anniversary Dark Side of the Moon Music Video Contest. My work is on extended loan at the Newport Art Museum. I was a recipient of an artist development grant from the Brown Arts Institute and was a member of their 2021 Cohort. I teach in the Film/Animation/Video department at Rhode Island School of Design.
